The Thoughtful Mind is a place for slow, careful writing about psychology — how the mind works, why we feel what we feel, and what research actually says once the headlines are stripped away.

New posts arrive regularly, covering topics from cognitive biases to the science of habit formation, written to be read thoughtfully rather than skimmed.

Thanks for reading.
